What I can do for you

Each project has a defined scope, a fixed quote before any work begins, and a deliverable you keep.

Audit

Price Leakage Audit

Twelve months of your transactions, measured against the prices you believe you charge.

  • Where discounts and overrides have drifted, and who is getting them
  • What each customer group and product line actually pays
  • How much margin slips away between list price and the invoice
  • A short, ranked list of fixes

Best for businesses with tiered, negotiated or salesperson-set pricing.

Margin

Margin & Cost Review

What every product, service or customer truly earns once the real costs are counted.

  • Landed cost where freight and overhead matter
  • Margin by line, by customer and by location
  • Items that quietly lose money, and items with room to move
  • An Excel margin model that stays with you

Best for businesses that know their revenue cold but not their margin.

Structure

Price Structure Build

A pricing framework that fits how you sell and protects the margin you need.

  • Customer tiers, price rules and discount guardrails
  • Promotions that never undercut your contract pricing
  • Targets tied to your margin goals, with changes rolled in steady steps
  • Documented and ready to load into your POS, ERP or spreadsheet

Best for growing businesses whose pricing grew up one exception at a time.

Launch

New Product & Service Pricing

Pricing a new line, a new location or a full menu of services, built from the ground up.

  • Cost build-up for each product or service
  • Competitor price research in your market
  • Price points set against your margin target
  • A simple rule for pricing whatever you add next

Best for launches, expansions and service businesses setting a menu.
